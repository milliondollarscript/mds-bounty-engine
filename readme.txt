Run public bounty and request boards with paid/featured posting fees, claims, and no-escrow moderation workflows.

== Description ==

Bounty Engine adds local-first bounty boards to Million Dollar Script. Site owners can accept public bounty requests, charge posting or featured fees, moderate listings, collect claimant submissions, and publish responsive boards with clear payment and reward disclosures.

Provider-mode listings remain private until both administrator approval and successful payment are confirmed. Manual-mode approval confirms an offline or free listing.

Public submissions require explicit terms acceptance and are checked against board capacity, moderation and payment visibility, valid calendar dates, HTTP/HTTPS links, claim deadlines, and an administrator-configurable hourly limit. Public and admin lists are paginated, and private data is covered by WordPress export, erasure, and scheduled retention tools.

The MVP does not include escrow, payouts, or custody of funds. Rewards, invoices, refunds, and delivery terms are handled directly by the parties unless the site owner provides separate terms.
